Section 1: Employee Leave and Attendance Policy
===========================================================

Employees are entitled to annual paid leave based on their years of service.

Employees with less than three years of service receive 18 paid leave days annually.

Employees with three to seven years of service receive 24 paid leave days annually.

Employees with more than seven years of service receive 30 paid leave days annually.

Sick leave is limited to 12 working days each calendar year.

Emergency leave requires approval from the reporting manager.

Any employee remaining absent without approval for more than three consecutive working days will automatically trigger an HR investigation.

Section 2: Working Hours and Remote Work Policy
===========================================================

The standard office working schedule is:

Monday to Friday

9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

One-hour lunch break

Employees are allowed to work remotely up to three days every week after manager approval.

Remote work requests exceeding three consecutive weeks require approval from the HR Director.

Employees working remotely must remain available through Microsoft Teams during office hours.

Repeated failure to respond during working hours may result in remote work privileges being revoked.

===========================================================

Domestic Flights:
Maximum ₹25,000

International Flights:
Maximum ₹1,50,000

Hotel:
₹8,000 per night

Meals:
₹2,000 per day

Taxi:
₹3,000 per trip

Travel requests above these limits require Finance Director approval.

Expense claims submitted after thirty calendar days will automatically be rejected.

Section 4: Vendor Payment Policy
===========================================================

Invoices less than ₹50,000 require Department Manager approval.

Invoices between ₹50,000 and ₹2,00,000 require Finance Manager approval.

Invoices exceeding ₹2,00,000 require CFO approval.

Wire transfers above ₹5,00,000 require dual authorization.

Purchase Orders must be created before invoice submission.

Vendor bank accounts may only be modified after finance verification.

Section 5: Information Security Policy
===========================================================

Passwords must contain:

Minimum 12 characters

One uppercase letter

One lowercase letter

One number

One special character

Passwords expire every 90 days.

Multi-factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all employees.

USB storage devices are prohibited unless specifically approved.

Employees must report phishing emails within one hour.

Section 6: Data Privacy Policy
===========================================================

Customer personal information shall never be shared externally.

Personally Identifiable Information (PII) includes:

Name

Phone Number

Email Address

Government ID

Passport Number

Credit Card Information

Sensitive customer records must remain encrypted both during storage and transmission.

Only authorized personnel may access confidential customer information.

Section 7: IT Asset Management
===========================================================

Every laptop shall receive an asset identification number.

Employees are responsible for company-issued devices.

Lost devices must be reported within two hours.

Damaged devices should be reported immediately.

Software installations require approval from the IT Department.

Operating system updates must never be postponed for more than fourteen days.

Section 8: Procurement Policy
===========================================================

Office equipment purchases below ₹25,000 may be approved by Team Leads.

Purchases between ₹25,000 and ₹1,00,000 require Procurement Manager approval.

Purchases exceeding ₹1,00,000 require Procurement Director approval.

Preferred vendors should always be selected whenever possible.

Competitive quotations are mandatory for purchases above ₹50,000.

Section 9: Incident Response Policy
===========================================================

Critical production incidents must be acknowledged within fifteen minutes.

High priority incidents must be acknowledged within thirty minutes.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA) documents must be completed within five business days.

Incident Severity Levels:

P1 Critical

P2 High

P3 Medium

P4 Low

Section 10: Financial Compliance
===========================================================

The maximum debt-to-equity ratio is 1.5.

Capital expenditure exceeding ₹50,00,000 requires Board approval.

Quarterly financial reports must be submitted within ten business days after quarter end.

Financial audits occur twice every year.

Internal audits may occur without notice.

Section 11: Artificial Intelligence Usage Policy
===========================================================

Employees may use approved AI tools for productivity.

Confidential company information shall never be uploaded into public AI systems.

All AI-generated content must undergo human review before publication.

Model outputs must never be treated as authoritative without verification.
