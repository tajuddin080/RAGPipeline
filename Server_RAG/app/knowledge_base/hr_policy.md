## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This Human Resources Policy Manual establishes the standards, procedures, and expectations that govern the employment relationship between ACME Corporation ("the Company") and its employees. It is designed to promote a productive, equitable, and respectful workplace while ensuring compliance with all applicable labour laws and regulations.

### 1.2 Scope

This policy applies to:

- All full-time permanent employees
- All part-time employees
- Contract employees on assignments exceeding 90 days
- Interns and apprentices under formal programmes

Consultants, freelancers, and third-party vendor staff are excluded from this policy unless explicitly stated.

### 1.3 Policy Updates

This manual is reviewed annually. Updates are communicated via official email and published on the internal HR portal. Employees are responsible for staying informed about the latest version. The most current version supersedes all previous versions.

## 2. Equal Opportunity and Non-Discrimination

### 2.1 Equal Opportunity Statement

ACME Corporation is an equal-opportunity employer. All employment decisions — including hiring, promotion, compensation, training, termination, and other conditions of employment — are made without regard to:

- Race, caste, colour, or national origin
- Gender, gender identity, or sexual orientation
- Age (except where a minimum age is a legal requirement)
- Religion or belief
- Disability or medical condition
- Marital or parental status
- Political opinion

### 2.2 Affirmative Action

The Company actively seeks to build a diverse workforce at all levels. Hiring managers are required to include at least one qualified candidate from an underrepresented group in every final shortlist of three or more candidates.

### 2.3 Reasonable Accommodation

Employees with disabilities or medical conditions may request reasonable accommodation by submitting a written request to HR. The Company will make accommodation decisions within 10 business days of receiving the request along with any required supporting documentation.

## 3. Code of Conduct

### 3.1 Professional Standards

All employees are expected to:

- Treat colleagues, clients, vendors, and visitors with dignity and respect
- Act honestly and with integrity in all business dealings
- Protect confidential information of the Company and its clients
- Avoid conflicts of interest or disclose them promptly
- Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and Company policies

### 3.2 Dress Code

Employees must maintain professional or business-casual attire in the office. The standard dress code is:

- **Monday to Thursday:** Business casual
- **Friday:** Smart casual (no torn clothing, beachwear, or offensive slogans)
- **Client meetings or presentations:** Business formal

### 3.3 Communication Standards

All written and verbal communications — whether internal or external — must be:

- Respectful and professional in tone
- Free from profanity, discriminatory language, and personal attacks
- Clear and factually accurate

Employees may not make public statements on behalf of ACME Corporation without prior approval from Corporate Communications.

### 3.4 Confidentiality

Employees have access to sensitive information as part of their roles. All employees must:

- Not disclose proprietary information to unauthorized individuals
- Sign the Non-Disclosure Agreement before their first day of work
- Immediately report suspected data breaches to the Information Security team

Confidentiality obligations persist for 24 months after the end of employment.

## 4. Compensation and Payroll

### 4.1 Salary Structure

ACME Corporation uses a band-based compensation structure with the following grade levels:

| Grade | Role Level | Band Label |
|-------|------------|------------|
| G1    | Analyst / Associate | Entry |
| G2    | Senior Analyst / Senior Associate | Junior |
| G3    | Lead / Specialist | Mid |
| G4    | Manager / Principal | Senior |
| G5    | Director | Leadership |
| G6    | Vice President / C-Suite | Executive |

Each grade has a defined salary band. Individual salaries are set within the band based on experience, skills, and market benchmarking.

### 4.2 Payroll Schedule

- Salaries are credited on the **last working day of every calendar month**.
- If the last day falls on a public holiday or weekend, payment is made on the preceding working day.
- Salary slips are issued electronically via the HR portal by the 2nd of the following month.

### 4.3 Allowances

Employees are eligible for the following allowances as applicable to their grade:

- **House Rent Allowance (HRA):** 40% of basic salary (metro cities), 30% (non-metro)
- **Transport Allowance:** ₹3,200 per month (Grade G1–G3), ₹5,000 per month (Grade G4+)
- **Medical Reimbursement:** Up to ₹15,000 per year (against bills)
- **Meal Vouchers:** ₹2,500 per month (non-taxable)

### 4.4 Overtime

- Employees in Grades G1–G3 are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5× their hourly rate for hours worked beyond 48 hours per week.
- Employees in Grades G4 and above are exempt from overtime pay; instead, they receive compensatory off with manager approval.

### 4.5 Payroll Deductions

Mandatory deductions include:

- Provident Fund (PF): 12% of basic salary
- Employee State Insurance (ESI): 0.75% of gross salary (applicable where gross ≤ ₹21,000)
- Professional Tax: As per applicable state rules
- Income Tax (TDS): Per Income Tax Act slabs, based on Form 16

## 5. Performance Management

### 5.1 Performance Review Cycle

ACME Corporation follows a bi-annual performance review cycle:

| Review Period | Assessment Window | Outcome Communicated |
|---------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Mid-Year Review | June 1 – June 30 | By July 15 |
| Annual Review | December 1 – December 31 | By January 20 |

### 5.2 Performance Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| 5 | Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all goals; role model behaviour |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | Regularly surpasses targets; strong competency demonstration |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | Fully achieves goals; solid, reliable contributor |
| 2 | Needs Improvement | Partially meets goals; gap areas identified |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | Fails to meet key goals; performance improvement plan required |

### 5.3 Goal Setting

- Goals must be set within **30 days of the start of each review cycle**.
- Goals follow the **SMART framework**: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
- Employees set a minimum of 3 and maximum of 7 goals per cycle.
- Goals are co-created with the reporting manager and endorsed via the HR system.

### 5.4 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

An employee rated "2 – Needs Improvement" for two consecutive review cycles or rated "1 – Unsatisfactory" in any single cycle may be placed on a **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)**:

- PIP duration: **60–90 calendar days**
- HR, the reporting manager, and the employee co-define measurable success criteria
- Weekly check-ins are mandatory
- Failure to meet PIP milestones may result in role reassignment, demotion, or termination

### 5.5 Promotions and Increments

- Promotions are based on sustained performance ratings of 4 or 5 for at least two consecutive cycles.
- Annual increments are released in **April** and are linked to the December annual review rating.
- Increment ranges by rating:

| Rating | Increment Range |
|--------|-----------------|
| 5 – Exceptional | 18% – 25% |
| 4 – Exceeds Expectations | 12% – 17% |
| 3 – Meets Expectations | 6% – 11% |
| 2 – Needs Improvement | 0% – 5% |
| 1 – Unsatisfactory | 0% |

## 6. Learning and Development

### 6.1 Training Budget

Each employee is allocated an annual Learning & Development (L&D) budget:

| Grade | Annual L&D Budget |
|-------|-------------------|
| G1 – G2 | ₹15,000 |
| G3 – G4 | ₹30,000 |
| G5 – G6 | ₹60,000 |

Unused L&D budget does not carry forward to the next year.

### 6.2 Mandatory Training

All employees must complete the following annually:

- **Code of Conduct and Ethics** (online, 90 minutes) — due by February 28
- **Information Security Awareness** (online, 60 minutes) — due by March 31
- **Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)** (online, 2 hours) — due by April 30
- **Data Privacy Fundamentals** (online, 60 minutes) — due by May 31

Failure to complete mandatory training within the deadline results in a temporary freeze of variable pay disbursement.

### 6.3 Internal Mobility

Employees may apply for internal job postings after completing **12 months** in their current role. The internal application process:

1. Employee notifies current manager (not for approval, but as professional courtesy)
2. Employee applies via the internal career portal
3. Interview process managed by Talent Acquisition
4. Offer requires sign-off from both receiving and current department heads

### 6.4 Mentorship Programme

ACME Corporation runs a structured 6-month mentorship programme twice annually (January and July cohorts). Employees at G1–G3 are eligible as mentees; employees at G4+ may volunteer as mentors.

## 7. Employee Benefits

### 7.1 Health Insurance

| Benefit | Coverage |
|---------|----------|
| Employee medical insurance | ₹5,00,000 per year |
| Spouse / domestic partner | ₹3,00,000 per year |
| Dependent children (up to age 25) | ₹2,00,000 per year each |
| Parents or in-laws (optional top-up) | ₹2,00,000 per year (50% premium paid by Company) |

Pre-existing conditions are covered after a 30-day waiting period.

### 7.2 Group Life Insurance

All permanent employees receive group term life insurance equivalent to **3× annual CTC** at no cost to the employee. Additional coverage up to 5× CTC may be purchased at a subsidised premium.

### 7.3 Gratuity

Employees completing **5 or more years of continuous service** are entitled to gratuity as per the Payment of Gratuity Act. The formula applied is:

> **Gratuity = (Last drawn basic salary × 15 × Years of service) ÷ 26**

### 7.4 Provident Fund

ACME Corporation contributes **12% of basic salary** to the Employee Provident Fund (EPF) on behalf of every eligible employee, matching the mandatory employee contribution.

### 7.5 Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)

Employees at Grade G4 and above are eligible for ESOPs:

- Vesting period: **4 years** (25% per year, cliff vest after Year 1)
- Exercise price: Set at fair market value on the grant date
- ESOP grants are subject to Board approval

### 7.6 Wellness Benefits

- Gym membership reimbursement: Up to ₹5,000 per month
- Annual health check-up: Fully covered (at empanelled hospitals)
- Employee Assistance Programme (EAP): 6 free confidential counselling sessions per year
- Ergonomic equipment allowance for remote workers: ₹10,000 one-time setup grant

## 8. Leave Entitlements

### 8.1 Leave Types Summary

| Leave Type | Entitlement | Carry Forward |
|------------|-------------|---------------|
| Earned Leave (EL) | 18–30 days (service-based) | Up to 15 days |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 12 days per year | No |
| Casual Leave (CL) | 8 days per year | No |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks (first 2 children) | N/A |
| Paternity Leave | 15 working days | No |
| Adoption Leave | 12 weeks (primary caregiver) | N/A |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 working days per incident | No |
| Study Leave | Up to 10 days per year | No |
| Sabbatical Leave | 1–3 months (after 7 years' service) | N/A |

### 8.2 Leave Application Process

- Leave requests must be submitted via the HR portal at least **3 working days in advance** for planned leave.
- Emergency leave may be communicated on the same day but must be followed by an application within 24 hours.
- Leave of more than **5 consecutive days** requires manager and HR approval.
- Planned leave during critical project phases may be deferred by the manager with HR notification.

### 8.3 Public Holidays

ACME Corporation observes **10 national public holidays** per year. Additionally, employees may choose **3 optional/restricted holidays** from a Company-published list of 7 dates.

### 8.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave

- **Maternity Leave:** Eligible after completing 80 days of work in the preceding 12 months. Fully paid for 26 weeks for first and second child; 12 weeks for third child onwards. Includes a nursing break of 2 × 15 minutes per day until the child turns 15 months.
- **Paternity Leave:** 15 working days to be availed within 6 months of childbirth or adoption.

## 9. Grievance Redressal

### 9.1 Definition

A grievance is any concern, complaint, or dissatisfaction that an employee formally raises relating to their work, work environment, or treatment at the workplace. Informal concerns raised verbally to a manager are not classified as grievances.

### 9.2 Filing a Grievance

An employee may file a grievance by:

1. Submitting the Grievance Form (available on the HR portal)
2. Sending a written email to hrgrievance@acmecorp.com
3. Submitting a sealed letter to the HR Head's office

Grievances must include: the nature of the complaint, names of parties involved, date of incident(s), and any evidence or witnesses.

### 9.3 Grievance Resolution Timeline

| Stage | Action | Timeline |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Acknowledgement | HR acknowledges receipt | Within 2 working days |
| Initial review | HR reviews and assigns | Within 5 working days |
| Investigation | HR investigates with parties | Within 15 working days |
| Resolution | Decision communicated | Within 20 working days |
| Appeal | Employee may appeal | Within 5 days of decision |

### 9.4 Confidentiality

All grievance proceedings are strictly confidential. Retaliation against an employee for filing a grievance in good faith is a serious disciplinary offence.

## 10. Disciplinary Policy

### 10.1 Disciplinary Actions

Disciplinary actions are progressive and proportionate to the severity of the misconduct:

| Level | Action | Examples |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Level 1 | Verbal Warning | Minor attendance lapses, minor policy deviations |
| Level 2 | Written Warning | Repeated Level 1 offences, insubordination |
| Level 3 | Final Written Warning | Serious misconduct, fraud attempt, harassment |
| Level 4 | Suspension (with or without pay) | Major policy violations, pending investigation |
| Level 5 | Termination | Gross misconduct, confirmed fraud, violence |

### 10.2 Gross Misconduct

Acts constituting gross misconduct leading to immediate termination include:

- Theft or misappropriation of Company property or funds
- Physical violence or threats against colleagues or visitors
- Sexual harassment
- Fabrication of documents or records
- Sharing confidential data with competitors
- Working for a direct competitor during employment without disclosure

### 10.3 Show-Cause Notice

Before any disciplinary action at Level 2 or above, the employee receives a **Show-Cause Notice** with:

- Clear description of the alleged misconduct
- Relevant evidence
- 5 working days to submit a written response

## 11. Anti-Harassment Policy

### 11.1 Zero Tolerance

ACME Corporation maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment in any form, including:

- **Sexual harassment** (as defined under the POSH Act, 2013)
- **Verbal harassment** (insults, belittling, public humiliation)
- **Psychological harassment** (bullying, exclusion, manipulation)
- **Digital harassment** (offensive messages, unwanted contact via digital channels)

### 11.2 Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)

The Company has constituted an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) as mandated under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013. The ICC:

- Has at least 50% female members
- Includes an external independent member from an NGO or legal background
- Meets within 7 days of receiving a complaint
- Completes inquiry within 90 days

### 11.3 Reporting

Complaints may be filed with the ICC via:

- ICC portal (accessible on the intranet)
- Sealed physical submission to any ICC member
- Anonymous ethics hotline: 1800-XXX-XXXX (toll-free, 24×7)

### 11.4 Protection of Complainant

The identity of the complainant is never disclosed. The complainant may request a temporary transfer to a different team during investigation. No adverse action may be taken against a complainant during or after the ICC process.

## 12. Workplace Health and Safety

### 12.1 Safe Work Environment

ACME Corporation is committed to providing a safe, healthy, and hazard-free workplace in compliance with the Factories Act and applicable state labour rules.

### 12.2 Emergency Procedures

- **Fire evacuation routes** are posted on every floor near stairwells.
- **Fire drills** are conducted twice a year.
- First aid kits are available at every floor reception and are restocked monthly.
- Emergency contact numbers are displayed near all primary exits.

### 12.3 Ergonomics

Employees spending more than 4 hours per day at a desk are entitled to:

- Ergonomic chair assessment (once per 2 years)
- Monitor stand or sit-stand desk upon HR-approved request
- Blue-light filtering glasses reimbursement up to ₹2,500

### 12.4 Mental Health

- All employees have access to the Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) with 6 free sessions per year.
- Mental health days (up to 2 per year) may be taken as part of Sick Leave without requiring a medical certificate.
- Managers are trained annually on psychological safety and stress recognition.

## 13. Social Media Policy

### 13.1 Personal Use

Employees may use social media on personal accounts and personal time. However, they must:

- Not disclose confidential, proprietary, or sensitive Company information
- Not post content that could embarrass or discredit the Company
- Not impersonate the Company or speak in the Company's name without authorisation
- Identify personal views as personal: use disclaimers like "Views are my own"

### 13.2 Professional Platforms

Employees representing the Company on platforms like LinkedIn must:

- Ensure their professional profile accurately reflects their current role
- Obtain approval from Corporate Communications before sharing press releases, announcements, or strategic updates
- Not engage in public disputes with clients, competitors, or the press

### 13.3 Violations

Social media violations may result in disciplinary action ranging from a written warning to termination, depending on severity and intent.

## 14. Conflict of Interest

### 14.1 Definition

A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal interests could — or could appear to — interfere with their professional duties to ACME Corporation.

### 14.2 Disclosure Requirements

Employees must disclose the following in writing to HR:

- Ownership of more than 5% equity in a competitor or supplier
- A close family member employed by a competitor, client, or vendor with whom the employee interacts
- Serving on the Board of Directors of an external company
- Receiving gifts, hospitality, or payments from vendors exceeding ₹5,000 in value per year

Disclosures must be made within **10 working days** of the conflict arising.

### 14.3 Gifts and Hospitality

| Item | Policy |
|------|--------|
| Gifts received < ₹1,000 | Acceptable; no disclosure needed |
| Gifts received ₹1,000 – ₹5,000 | Must be declared to manager |
| Gifts received > ₹5,000 | Must be returned or handed to HR |
| Cash gifts | Never acceptable; must be returned |
